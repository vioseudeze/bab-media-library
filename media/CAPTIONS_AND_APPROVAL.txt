Feeling overstimulated doesn't mean you love your baby less. Create a 15-minute pause: lower the noise, hand over responsibility, and reset.

Rest can mean fewer demands, not another task.

Sat 24 Oct | Decision fatigue
INSTAGRAM:
Even choosing dinner can feel like too much.

New motherhood involves countless small decisions nobody sees. Have a helper offer a specific choice: 'I'll cook pasta or rice—pick one.'

Better still: let them own the whole task.
